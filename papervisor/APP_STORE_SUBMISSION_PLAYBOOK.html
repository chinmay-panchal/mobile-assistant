Please keep the app installed for 14 days and test creating an exam paper or searching for past year papers. If you encounter any bugs, reply directly to this message. Thank you for your help!

Apple TestFlight

Unlike Google Play, Apple TestFlight does not require 20 testers for 14 continuous days. Internal testing is available immediately. External beta testing requires standard automated Beta App Review (typically approved within hours).
