UK GDPR & DPA 2018 PECR Compliant Consolidated Legal Terms

Privacy Policy, UK GDPR Disclosures & Terms of Service

Last Updated & Effective: October 2026

This consolidated legal notice outlines how Papervisor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, secures, processes, and protects personal data in full compliance with the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018), and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), along with the binding terms governing the use of the Papervisor mobile application and web services.

1. Data Controller Information

Papervisor is an educational assessment design suite engineered for teachers, school administrators, and academic institutions to generate syllabus-aligned exam papers, worksheets, and question repositories.

  • Data Controller: Papervisor Education Ltd.
  • Contact / Data Protection Officer (DPO): info@qrioustech.com
  • Governing Jurisdiction: England and Wales (United Kingdom).
  • Supervisory Authority: UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF (ico.org.uk).

2. Personal Data We Collect (Data Minimisation)

In accordance with UK GDPR Article 5(1)(c) (Data Minimisation), we collect and process only the minimal information strictly required to operate the service:

  • Account Identifiers: Full name, institutional or professional email address, and cryptographically salted password hashes (never stored in plaintext).
  • Curriculum & Workspace Data: Subject titles, grade levels, uploaded academic source documents (PDF textbooks, course modules), past year questions (PYQs), and user-generated question papers.
  • Technical Session State: JWT session authentication tokens stored exclusively in encrypted device storage (FlutterSecureStorage on iOS/Android, secure storage on Web).
  • What We Do NOT Collect: We never collect student personal identifiable information (PII), student records, biometrics, or special category data under UK GDPR Article 9. We do not sell personal data to advertisers or commercial brokers.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing (UK GDPR Article 6)

We process personal data under the following statutory lawful grounds:

  • Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): To authenticate authorized educators, maintain subject workspaces, and deliver requested examination generation services.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Maintaining operational availability, server security, abuse prevention, and diagnostic reliability.
  • Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Granted by the educator upon registration and when requesting AI-powered question synthesis.

4. AI Sub-Processors & International Data Transfers

Papervisor uses state-of-the-art artificial intelligence models to assist educators in question drafting and curriculum lookup:

  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC): Processes teacher-selected curriculum excerpts to synthesize structured examination questions and marking guides. Customer data is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encryption and is not used to train Google's public foundation models.
  • Cloud Hosting (AWS EC2): Self-contained server architecture hosting PostgreSQL (pgvector) and Redis caching with strict firewall rules and TLS/SSL enforcement.
  • International Transfer Safeguards: Cross-border data transfers outside the UK comply with UK GDPR Article 46 using the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

5. Data Retention, Storage & Security

  • Security Controls: All data transmissions enforce HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption. Backend logs are scrubbed to strip authentication tokens and credentials. Client session tokens utilize OS-level hardware encryption (Keychain / KeyStore).
  • Data Retention: Account data is retained solely during the active life of your account. When an account is deleted, all associated personal records and workspace items are permanently expunged within 30 days.
  • Local Cache Clearing: Temporary PDF render files on mobile devices can be erased at any time through the in-app "Clear Cache" button or by logging out.

6. Your Statutory Rights Under UK GDPR

Data subjects in the United Kingdom hold full statutory rights under the UK GDPR:

  • Right to Access (Art. 15): Obtain confirmation of whether your data is processed and receive a portable copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Update inaccurate or incomplete profile information.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Permanently delete your account and all associated workspace records. This can be executed self-service inside the application or by emailing our privacy team.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18): Restrict processing under contested circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Export your questions, papers, and curriculum assets in standardized formats.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint directly with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Account & Data Deletion Request (Google Play & Apple Compliance)

Users may request full deletion of their account and all personal data through either method:

  1. In-App Self-Service: In the Papervisor application, open the Profile Drawer / Settings and tap "Delete Account". Confirm your decision to immediately purge all tokens and initiate account erasure.
  2. Direct Request: Email our privacy team at info@qrioustech.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address.

Data Deleted: Profile details (name, email), authentication records, user-created workspaces, uploaded textbook chapters/reference PDFs, and generated exam papers. Data deletion is completed within 48 hours of receipt.
